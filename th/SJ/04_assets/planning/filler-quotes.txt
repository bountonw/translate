# SJ filler quotations from the 2023 print

The 2023 print filled the ends of these chapters with quotations that are not in the English book. They stood in the chapter files as lines commented out with //, after the paragraph named, and were removed so that the chapter files hold only the book. Each line is copied as it stood. The last entry of th/SJ/04_assets/planning/SIDEQUESTS.md decides at typesetting whether any chapter takes a filler; delete this file when that is done.

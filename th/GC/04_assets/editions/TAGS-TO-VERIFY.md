# Paragraph tags to verify against a printed English Great Controversy

Six paragraph numbers in the Thai printed edition disagree with
`lo/GC/00_source/`, and two headings in the English source disagree with the
paragraphs beneath them. None of it can be settled from the files alone,
because a disagreement between two Thai books and one English file could mean
either that both Thai books are wrong or that the English file is.

For each case below, open the English page named and count the paragraphs on
it. The question is always the same: does that page carry as many paragraphs
as `lo/GC/00_source/` says it does?

The Lao manuscripts are not in question. All 43 of them carry exactly the tags
their English source carries, so whatever is decided here applies to the
English source and the Lao translation together.

## Case A — English page 150, chapter 8

**What to count:** English page 150 should carry 5 paragraphs, numbered 150.1, 150.2, 150.3, 150.4, 150.5.

**What the Thai books do:** The printed Thai edition ends two consecutive paragraphs with {GC 150.3}. The other Thai edition does the same. Neither carries {GC 150.4}.

**The paragraphs, as `lo/GC/00_source/` has them:**

- `{GC 150.1}` — A more able and forcible denunciation of the papal abuses could not have been presented by Luther himself; …
- `{GC 150.2}` — Had the eyes of the assembly been opened, they would have beheld angels of God in the midst …
- `{GC 150.3}` — A committee was at once appointed by the Diet to prepare an enumeration of the papal oppressions that …
- `{GC 150.4}` — The council now demanded the Reformer’s appearance before them. Notwithstanding the entreaties, protests, and threats of Aleander, the …
- `{GC 150.5}` — The friends of Luther were terrified and distressed. Knowing the prejudice and enmity against him, they feared that …

**If page 150 really has 5 paragraphs**, the English source is right and both Thai books mislabel one of them; the Thai files get `{GC 150.4}` restored.

**If page 150 has fewer**, `lo/GC/00_source/GC08_en.md` has split one paragraph into two, and the Lao manuscript `lo/GC/03_public/GC08_lo.md` has to be corrected with it.

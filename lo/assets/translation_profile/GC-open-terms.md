DEFER-NOTE "London ed." in D'Aubigné footnote citations — book-wide, apparatus only, not a term. EGW's 1911 text tags certain D'Aubigné quotations *London ed.* because the default is the American Tract Society translation, the only one the author approved; the tag marks the exception. The editions differ in wording, not in book/chapter numbering, so the tag aids verification rather than location. The Lao already carries it in three wordings: ສະບັບຈັດພິມໃນນະຄອນລອນດອນ (GC07 [^22], ເຫຼັ້ມ 4 ບົດ 8), ສະບັບຕີພິມຢູ່ນະຄອນ ລອນດອນ (GC09 [^16], ເຫຼັ້ມ 8 ບົດ 11; also a stray space inside ນະຄອນ ລອນດອນ), ສະບັບຕີພິມທີ່ລອນດອນ (GC12 [^3], ເຫຼັ້ມ 12 ບົດ 2). GC 209.1 [^24] omits the tag where the printed English carries it and the online transcription does not; printed reading preferred, since an edition tag is dropped by transcribers rather than invented by editors. GC07 [^32] is a full imprint (edition, year, printer), a different form — out of scope, do not fold in. Two decisions, both corpus-wide: (a) carry the tag or drop it everywhere; (b) if carried, one standard wording — ສະບັບຕີພິມທີ່ລອນດອນ recommended as shortest and cleanest read-aloud. Grep "London ed" in GC_en_full.md and ລອນດອນ in the Lao footnote lines to build the inventory first. Do not adjudicate in batch.

it may only hold the peace until the body that can — the church council —
convenes. Two different Lao words are required or they say the Diet must wait
for the Diet.

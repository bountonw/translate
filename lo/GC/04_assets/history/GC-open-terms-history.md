# GC open terms — decision history

Why this file exists. gc-batch-auditor 2.B reads GC-open-terms.md for every
ref in a batch's range, so that file is loaded on every batch dispatch and
its length is a tax the whole audit pays. An entry there carries only the
decision an agent must act on. The reasoning behind it, the corpus counts,
the per-site ref lists and the record of how the deferral was closed live
here instead.

Nothing here is deleted content. It is content that moved.

How to read it. Blocks are keyed by the exact entry name as it stands in
GC-open-terms.md, with its type prefix — DEFER-TERM, EXCEPT-TERM, NOTE-TERM,
NOTE-SPELL or DEFER-NOTE — and the chapter at which each ruling was made, so
a later QA pass can tell when a decision was taken and against what evidence.

Who reads it. People, not agents. lo/GC/CLAUDE.md 2.G puts this directory on
the never-read list.

## DEFER-TERM ຄັ່ງສາສະໜາ without ໄຄ້ for "fanatic / fanatical" — closed by Brian at GC 22

The ruling. Brian judged the shortened form an acceptable alternate reading
and directed that it be added to the glossary Fanatic row as an option. The
four GC 22 markers were therefore removed without any change to the text,
and the deferral was deleted from GC-open-terms.md. The Fanatic row now
reads ພວກທີ່ຄັ່ງໄຄ້ / ຄວາມຄັ່ງໄຄ້ / ພວກຄັ່ງສາສະໜາ / ຄົນຄັ່ງສາສະໜາ and carries
"never mark", so the pre-pass stops flagging the family.

The evidence the deferral carried. GC 13 renders one English word three ways
inside two paragraphs: ກຸ່ມຄົນທີ່ຖືສາສະໜາແບບພ້ຽນໆ, then ພວກຄັ່ງໄຄ້ສາສະໜາ, then
ພວກຄັ່ງສາສະໜາ at 239.1, and ພວກຊາວເມືອງມຸນສະເຕີທີ່ຄັ່ງສາສະໜາ at 239.2. The
shortened form was not local to that chapter: 12 occurrences across GC05 (1),
GC13 (2), GC14 (2), GC22 (4), GC28 (1) and GC37 (2). GC 22's four sites were
395.1, 396.1, 397.1 and 398.1, marked in the QA2 run as family #4a to #4d.

What the ruling does not cover. GC 18 adds ຄົນບ້າສາສະໜາ at 336.2, one site,
in a sneering register. That form is built on ບ້າ rather than on ຄັ່ງ, so
Brian's decision about the ຄັ່ງ family does not reach it and it remains
unadjudicated. It was raised to him separately in the GC 22 report.

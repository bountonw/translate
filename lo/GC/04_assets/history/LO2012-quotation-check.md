# LO2012 quotation check — the sites that do not match

The book's introduction lists the Lao printed edition of 2012 and 2015 as the version whose quotations carry no label in a citation. Two texts of that family exist: LO2012, the online text at `~/programming/bible/LO2012/`, and LO2015, the printed edition, which the translator has in hand and typed some verses from. This file lists the places where the book's quotation does not match LO2012, so that each can be checked against the printed edition and the introduction's version note settled.

Regenerate it with `python3 lo/GC/04_assets/scripts/gc_versecheck.py --show DIFFERS,NO-BOOK,CLOSE`.

Of the 470 unlabelled quotations in the book, 312 appear in LO2012 word for word and 113 more have 95 per cent or more of their wording there, so 425 are LO2012 beyond question. The rest are below. The two texts spell the same words differently — the online export writes ຫລ, ຫນ, ຫມ and the decomposed AM vowel where the book writes ຫຼ, ໜ, ໝ and the composed AM — so the script normalises both sides and no site listed here is a spelling difference.

Two of the thirteen below are not defects. At GC14 {GC 259.3} and GC21 {GC 388.1} the quoted passage is a long extract from an author that has scripture quoted inside it, and the citation names only the scripture, so the comparison is measuring the whole extract against three verses. The one NO-BOOK line, GC27 {GC 473.2}, is a two-word cross-reference rather than a quotation.

    LO2012 : ດ້ວຍວ່າ, ຈະມີສຽງຮ້ອງບັນຊາອັນດັງ, ສຽງຂອງອັກຄະເທວະດາ ສຽງແກຂອງພຣະເຈົ້າ ແລະອົງພຣະຜູ້ເປັນເຈົ້າເອງຈະສະເດັດລົງມາຈາກສະຫວັນ ພວກທີ່ເຊື່ອໃນພຣະຄຣິດທີ່ຕາຍໄປແລ້ວ ກໍຈະເປັນຄືນມາສູ່ຊີວິດກ່ອນ.  ແລ້ວພວກເຮົາຜູ້ທີ່ຍັງມີຊີວິດຢູ່ໃນເວລານັ້ນ ກໍຈະຖືກຮັບຂຶ້ນໄປໃນເມກພ້ອມກັບຄົນພວກນັ້ນ ເພື່ອພົບກັບອົງພຣະຜູ້ເປັນເຈົ້າໃນຟ້າອາກາດ. ຢ່າງນັ້ນແຫຼະ ພວກເຮົາຈຶ່ງຈະຢູ່ກັບອົງພຣະຜູ້ເປັນເຈົ້າຕະຫລອດໄປເປັນນິດ.  ດ້ວຍເຫດນີ້ ຈົ່ງເລົ້າໂລມອອຍໃຈຊຶ່ງກັນແລະກັນ ດ້ວຍຂໍ້ຄວາມເຫຼົ່ານີ້ເທີ້ນ.

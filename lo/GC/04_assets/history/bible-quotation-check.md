# Bible quotation check — the quotations whose wording is not a Lao Bible

The book's introduction lists the Lao printed edition of 2012 and 2015 as the version whose quotations carry no label in a citation. Two Lao texts of that family sit under `~/programming/bible/`: LO2012, the online text, and LCV. Both cover the whole Bible. The Thai and English directories hold only the gospels and a few other books, and a Lao quotation cannot be compared against a Thai text in any case, so those are not used here.

Regenerate this file with `python3 lo/GC/04_assets/scripts/gc_versecheck.py --report`.

Spelling is standardised on both sides before anything is compared, so nothing below is a spelling difference. Three things are levelled. The Bible exports write ຫລ, ຫນ, ຫມ and decompose the AM vowel where the book writes ຫຼ, ໜ, ໝ and composes it. Every wrong-to-right pair the repository already records is applied to both sides: 531 of them, from `.tooling/forbidden_terms/lao.txt`, `lo/assets/dictionaries/common-spelling.txt` and section 10 of the GC glossary, with ຣ and ລ treated as one letter. And thirteen further spellings are the book's own, each checked against all 42 chapters as a form the book writes one way everywhere and the other way nowhere: ຄຣິສ for ຄຣິດ, ສະບາໂຕ for ຊະບາໂຕ, ຍຸຕິທຳ for ຍຸດຕິທຳ, ຄາຕະກຳ for ຄາດຕະກຳ, ໂຮ່ຮ້ອງ for ໂຮຮ້ອງ, ເຍາະເຍີ້ຍ for ເຢາະເຢີ້ຍ, ດົນຕີ for ດົນຕຼີ, ປຶ້ມ for ປື້ມ, ສິລາ for ສີລາ, ເຄິ່ງ for ເຄີ່ງ and ເຊິ່ງ for ຊຶ່ງ and ຊິ່ງ. No word is levelled where the book writes it both ways, and no synonym is levelled at all, because folding one away would hide a difference of wording.

Of 454 unlabelled quotations, 359 are word for word in LO2012 and 17 more are LO2012 in the book's own spelling. The other 78 differ in wording and are below, in Bible order from Genesis to Revelation.

A match has to be exact. Where a quotation is elided with an ellipsis, every part between the ellipses has to be found, because that is what the ellipsis claims. Differing words are wrapped in **stars** on both sides, each run widened to the spaces around it so that a mark never cuts a Lao word in half, and a run that turns up unchanged in the other text is never marked. Where a verse is much longer than the quotation, only the stretch the quotation lines up with is shown, with an ellipsis at the cut. Each heading says how many letters the nearer of the two Lao texts differs by, and the smallest are indexed first.

One difference recurs and is a wording choice rather than a spelling. Where both Lao Bibles write the divine name ພຣະເຈົ້າຢາເວ, the book sometimes writes ອົງພຣະຜູ້ເປັນເຈົ້າ instead. The book does use ພຣະເຈົ້າຢາເວ, at 78 sites, so this is decided place by place and those quotations stay in the list.

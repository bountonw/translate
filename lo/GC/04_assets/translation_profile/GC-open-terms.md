The approving rule in each row is a pattern rather than a closed list, because the forms are built compositionally and a new chapter will always be able to build another one: a person-head (ພວກ, ຄົນ or ຜູ້, alone or with ທີ່) plus a word of allegiance (ຝ່າຍ, ນິຍົມ, ເຄັ່ງໃນ, ສະໜັບສະໜູນ or ໃນ) plus the institution. NOTE-TERM Clergy — DECIDED and CLOSED at GC 15, no longer an open question; the evidence below is kept as the record of how it was reached. The row sorts by confession and not by social role: Catholic → ບາດຫຼວງ, or ຜູ້ນຳຄຣິສຕະຈັກ where the English names the leadership corps; Protestant and modern → ສາສະໜາຈານ; NOT ນັກບວດ, which GC-clergy-fixes.md reserves for a monk. There is no separate estate sense, because the French First Estate is Catholic and takes ບາດຫຼວງ like every other Catholic site, so the former "estate → ນັກບວດ" branch was deleted from the row rather than re-pointed. Earlier evidence, seen: 118.1 (×2) as ບາດຫຼວງ, 148.1 as ນັກບວດ ("clergy and laity" → ພວກນັກບວດ ແລະ ປະຊາຊົນ), 214.3 as ບາດຫຼວງ ("clergy and the people"). Adjudicate row wording corpus-wide. GC 15 adds eight estate-sense sites and they do not agree with one another. In two consecutive paragraphs: 279.3 ("her clergy", listed beside her king and her legislators) is ອາຈານສອນສາສະໜາ, a form the glossary row does not list at all and which reads as men who teach religion rather than as the First Estate; 279.4 ("heavily taxed by the civil authorities and by the clergy") is ນັກບວດ; and 279.4 again, inside the quoted passage ("by the secular magnates on the one hand, and the clergy on the other"), is ບາດຫຼວງ. The two forms inside 279.4 name one body twice in one paragraph, so a listener hears two taxing bodies where the source has one. Pages 280 to 284 add five more occurrences, and the chapter's own majority is ບາດຫຼວງ: 281.2 ("They beheld the clergy as a party to their oppression") is ບາດຫຼວງ, and the preceding "priestcraft" in the same sentence is ບາດຫຼວງ too, so the paragraph is internally consistent; 282.1 ("a representation exceeding that of the nobles and the clergy combined", the Three Estates at the Estates-General, which is the estate sense at its most explicit) is ບາດຫຼວງ; 283.1 ("King, clergy, and nobles were compelled to submit") is ນັກບວດ; 283.2 ("The example of persecution which the clergy of France had exhibited") is ນັກບວດ; and 283.2 again ("the Roman Catholic clergy experienced all those woes") is ບາດຫຼວງ. The 283.2 pair repeats the 279.4 problem in a sharper form: both occurrences sit inside one quoted passage from Wylie describing one body, with "the priests" (rendered ບາດຫຼວງ) between them — that phrase is inside the quoted passage as well rather than EGW's own wording, so all three occurrences in the paragraph are one author's — and a listener hears the monks persecuting and the priests suffering for it. Counting the chapter, GC 15 renders the estate sense ບາດຫຼວງ at 279.4 (quoted passage), 281.2, 282.1 and 283.2 (second), ນັກບວດ at 279.4 (EGW's own sentence), 283.1 and 283.2 (first), and ອາຈານສອນສາສະໜາ once at 279.3. The chapter therefore leans to ບາດຫຼວງ, which is also what the GC-clergy-fixes.md governing rule leaves available once ນັກບວດ is reserved for monks, and 118.1 already stands at ບາດຫຼວງ. All eight sites are marked inline in the manuscript as #12a through #12h in text order — 279.3, 279.4 twice, 281.2, 282.1, 283.1 and 283.2 twice — each carrying its own evidence and the standing question, rather than a single marker standing in for the family. GC 15 has a ninth occurrence of the English word, at 272.3 ("the exultation among the clergy knew no bounds"), which is the Roman clergy rejoicing at the news of the massacre and not the French estate; it is rendered ພວກບາດຫຼວງ, which is what the Clergy row prescribes for the Catholic sense, and it is not evidence for the estate question and should not be swept into a corpus grep of that family. The decision was taken on a corpus sweep of all 34 occurrences of English "clergy": Catholic in GC 03, 05, 06, 07, 08, 10, 12 and 15 and at GC 596.3; Protestant in GC 14, 16 and 20; modern in GC 37 and 38. Every Protestant and modern site already read ສາສະໜາຈານ and none was touched. Five sites change to ບາດຫຼວງ — GC 279.3, 279.4, 283.1 and 283.2 in this chapter as markers 12a, 12b, 12f and 12g, and GC 148.1 in chapter 8 as that chapter's marker 1, the only site outside GC 15 where ນັກບວດ rendered "clergy". Three single-site variants are licensed rather than normalised away, and are recorded in the Clergy row: ອາຈານສອນສາສະໜາ at GC 596.3, where the clergy are named as the teaching authority competent to interpret Scripture and ບາດຫຼວງ carries "ecclesiastics" in the next clause; ອາຈານປະຈຳໂບດ at GC 257.2 for Anglican parish clergy, licensed by the English "in their own parishes"; and ຜູ້ຮັບໃຊ້ຂອງໂບດ at GC 221.2, which has its own entry below. Do not reopen these sites.

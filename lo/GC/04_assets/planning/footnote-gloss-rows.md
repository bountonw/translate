# Footnote author-gloss sweep — what was done and what was left alone

The sweep gave the English name to every author cited in a footnote. It first glossed all 174 author citations, and on 30 August the translator ruled that the gloss stands once per author per chapter, at that chapter's first citation of them, so 97 repeats were stripped again. Martyn is the one exception and keeps its gloss at every occurrence, because the Lao form ມາຕິນ is a homonym of ມາຕິນ ລູເທີ, Martin Luther, and a bare citation is otherwise ambiguous; that exception is Martyn's alone and is not extrapolated to any other name. The book carried 26 glossed footnotes before the sweep and carries 114 now. Sixteen of the names glossed had no row in section 11 of `lo/GC/04_assets/translation_profile/GC-glossary.txt`, and the translator directed that they be added; they now stand at the end of that table. The rows are repeated below as the record of where each English form came from.

Every English form below was taken from the English source at the anchor the citation sits under, and none was transliterated from the Lao. No conflict was found: no row in section 11 gives a different Lao form for any of these names.

Sixteen footnote heads were judged out of scope and left unglossed, because section 4 of the sweep brief limits the gloss to a person's name serving as an author citation. They are the two explanatory notes on the German empire at {GC 57.3} and {GC 138.1}; the Wolff journal title at three sites in GC20, settled on 13 August; the Tertullian Apology title at {GC 41.3}, which GC13 already glosses as a work; the periodicals whose names already stand in Latin — *Journal of Paris*, *The Essex Antiquarian*, *Blackwood's Magazine*, *Congregational Journal* and Second Advent Library; the encyclopedias *Encyclopedia Americana* and *Encyclopaedia Britannica*; the United States Congressional documents at {GC 295.3}; the newspaper article "The Old Countryman" at {GC 333.2}; the Dublin Nation at {GC 440.2}; the book *The Healthy Christian* at {GC 387.1}, whose source names no author; and the Plymouth speech at {GC 440.2}, whose source names no author either.

## The two chapters QA3 has not yet read

QA3 has a record for GC01 to GC30 and none for GC31 to GC42, which is group 6. Twelve of the sweep's sites fall in that group, so those paragraphs enter the group 6 packet as changed text although QA1 and QA2 never touched them. The translator ruled on 30 August that this is not worth avoiding: the QA3 reader will either object to the gloss or judge it STANDS, and either answer is useful. The sites are GC33 at {GC 547.1} and {GC 549.2}, twice at the second, and GC35 at {GC 564.1}, {GC 565.2}, {GC 571.2}, {GC 574.2}, {GC 575.1}, {GC 575.2}, {GC 577.1} and {GC 580.3}, twice at the last.
